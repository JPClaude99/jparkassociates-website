July 2026, before it starts.

July is the heaviest CDTFA month of the year for most distributors: the Q2 prepayment lands on the 24th and the full Q2 return follows on the 31st. Local wage increases hit on the 1st. Here’s the full picture.

Deadlines this month

If we handle your books or payroll, the ones in our lane are already on our calendar.

Wed, Jul 1 Local minimum-wage increases take effect — employers with staff in City of LA, unincorporated LA County, or Pasadena
Wed, Jul 15 Federal & CA payroll tax deposit for June payroll — monthly-schedule depositors
Fri, Jul 24 CDTFA sales-tax prepayment for July — prepayment accounts
Fri, Jul 31 Form 941 — Q2 payroll tax return — all employers
Fri, Jul 31 CDTFA Q2 sales & use tax return — quarterly filers
Fri, Jul 31 FUTA deposit if liability over $500 — employers

What changed

Local minimum wages go up July 1. The City of Los Angeles moves to $18.42 / hour, unincorporated LA County to $18.47, and Pasadena to $18.57. Glendale has no local ordinance and follows California’s statewide rate ($16.90), which does not change July 1. Verify the rate for each warehouse or facility location before the first July payroll.

No new district sales-tax rate changes announced for LA County for July. Confirm before sending: check the CDTFA Special Notices page for any district rate changes that could affect delivered sales in your service area, especially if you sell into multiple LA-area cities.

For your industry

Mid-year resale certificate hygiene

A CDTFA audit can turn into a sales-tax liability if you can’t produce a valid resale certificate for every exempt sale. Mid-year is a low-pressure time to tighten this up:

  • Pull a list of customers you sold to in Q1 and Q2 without collecting tax — does each one have a current certificate on file?
  • Chase down any missing or expired certificates now, before year-end when customers are harder to reach
  • For new customers onboarded since January, confirm the certificate was collected before the first sale shipped

Local notes

If you deliver into multiple LA-area cities, sales tax is sourced to the delivery location — and district rates vary city by city. The July 1 wage increases affect warehouse and dock staff at each facility. Verify the applicable minimum-wage rate for each location where employees physically work.
