What changed

Local minimum wages adjust July 1. The City of Los Angeles, unincorporated L.A. County, and several nearby cities raise their rates on the first — confirm the new rate for each location where your staff actually work before the first July payroll run.

The federal tip deduction is in effect for 2026. Your staff can deduct up to $25,000 of reported tips on their returns — which makes accurate W-2 tip data worth real money to them. We covered the employer side in The Ledger.

For your industry

Summer hires: set up tip reporting on day one

Every seasonal hire who clears $20 in tips a month has a reporting duty — and you have withholding duties built on top of it. Onboarding is the cheap moment to get this right:

  • Add new hires to the POS tip capture before their first shift
  • Show them the cash-tip report once — it takes five minutes
  • Mid-July, spot-check that reported tips look plausible against card tips

Local notes

Minimum wage depends on where the work happens, not your mailing address: a La Crescenta address can sit in unincorporated county territory or City of Glendale territory street by street. If you’re unsure which rate applies to a location, send us the address and we’ll confirm it.
